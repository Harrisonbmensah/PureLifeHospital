Executive summary

Good outcomes, concentrated demand

The hospital generated about $294.03M in revenue from 5,000 admissions, with an average length of stay of 3.56 days and a mortality rate of 2.22%. Most patients are successfully discharged, which points to generally positive clinical outcomes. However, demand is concentrated in specific diagnoses and visit types, creating resource-allocation and capacity-planning challenges.

Business interpretation

Outpatient visits are about 50% of all admissions, making them the hospital's primary service channel.

Recommendation

  • Prioritise staffing and scheduling for outpatient departments.
  • Expand outpatient capacity during peak periods.
  • Use appointment optimisation to cut wait times and improve throughput.

Conclusion

Where to act

Pure Life Hospital generated $294.03M from 5,000 admissions while keeping mortality low at 2.22% and length of stay at 3.56 days. Outpatient services account for half of all admissions and are the main demand driver. Digestive system disorders are the largest diagnostic category, an opportunity for targeted resource allocation and preventive care.
